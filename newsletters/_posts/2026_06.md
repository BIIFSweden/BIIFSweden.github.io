# Project highlight 

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://biifsweden.github.io/images/newsletter/2026_06/ProjectHighlight_2026_05_06_8376.gif" width="500" alt="Project image">
</p>

**Maximilian Senftleben** from the BIIF team developed Pyflux, a desktop Graphical User Interface for loading, filtering, visualizing, aligning, clustering, and exporting MINFLUX (Minimal fluorescence photon flux microscopy) localization data. Max built the tool with PySide6, Plotly, Pandas, SciPy, and Zarr.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://biifsweden.github.io/images/newsletter/2026_06/vid.gif" width="500" alt="Project image">
</p>

- Filter localizations by trace length, EFO, and CFR
- View data in interactive 2D/3D scatter plots
- Compare datasets in a multicolor viewer
- Generate heatmaps, Gaussian renderings, and TIFF exports
- Align datasets using MBM bead-based registration
- Run DBSCAN clustering on track-averaged localizations
- Export filtered data and summary statistics to CSV

The tool has been developed as a collaboration with Ana Agostinho and Bruno Stojcic from the Advanced Light Microscopy Facility (ALM) at Scilifelab Solna. ALM has recently added MINFLUX to their variety of imaging methods and pyflux will therefore be used not only by the ALM scientists but also by users of the facility. Pyflux can be installed on Windows or Mac and can be downloaded from [here](https://github.com/BIIFSweden/8376_minflux) under the current releases. 
 
If you have similar microscopy images or any image analysis questions feel free to reach us at biif@scilifelab.se or submit your project requests at https://nbis.se/services/bioimage-informatics. See more of our work on the [BIIF projects page](https://biifsweden.github.io/).
