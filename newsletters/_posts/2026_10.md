## Images to Knowledge (I2K) 2026

The BIIF team, together with Leslie Solorzano from NBIS, held a virtual workshop titled "Integration of multi-modal data and visualization using TissUUmaps 4".
The workshop covered the basics of image registration, working with SpatialData, and aligning spatial transcriptomics data with light microscopy images. A final session showed how to visualize the registered multi-modal data in the newly released TissUUmaps 4.



## Elixir Spatial Omics course 

<p align="center">

**Christophe Avenel** from BIIF co-organised the ELIXIR Spatial Omics Data Analysis course, held at BMC in Uppsala on 6–9 th October 2026, hosted by NBIS together with the ELIXIR Single-Cell Omics community. The four-day course brought together trainers from across Europe to teach participants how to analyse spatially resolved transcriptomics data. Besides organising, Christophe taught two lectures: one on cell segmentation and data formats, together with Jonas Windhager, and one on imaging-based spatial data analysis. Jonas led a practical on spatial data formats, and Suganya Sivagurunathan led the hands-on cell segmentation practical. 

All the course materials are available on this [page](https://elixir-europe-training.github.io/ELIXIR-SCO-spatial-omics-2026/schedule.html).


# Publication highlight 

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://biifsweden.github.io/images/newsletter/2026_07/PublicationHighlight.png" width="500" alt="Project image">
</p>

 In this paper, O´Roberts et al. present a protocol for large tissue sections that combines multiplexed detection of genes using DNA FISH with multiplexed immunofluorescence detection of proteins on the PhenoCycler Fusion platform in large tissue sections. **Christophe Avenel** from BIIF built the image analysis pipeline, which segments cells, measures protein marker intensities and counts DNA FISH spots in each cell. The data can be explored interactively in [TissUUmaps](https://is-pla.serve.scilifelab.se/TissUUmaps_project_1.tmap?path=dna_fish)!


Read the full publication here: [Eleanor M O Roberts, Pranauti Panshikar, Xiaoze Li Wang, Christophe Avenel, Quentin Verron, Eléna Coulier, Magda Bienko, Charlotte Stadler (2026). An integrated protocol for multiplexed DNA FISH and protein detection in large tissue sections. Journal of Histotechnology, 49(3), 199–215. https://doi.org/10.1080/01478885.2026.2703359](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01478885.2026.2703359)

Explore the code: https://github.com/BIIFSweden/6870_DNA_isPLA

## GloBIAS 2026 workshop 

The GloBIAS 2026 Bioimage Analysts' Workshop takes place on 10–13 November 2026 at ISAS Campus in Dortmund, Germany.
This year's theme is on how AI is changing bioimage analysis. Sessions cover AI foundation models, AI agents with live demos, where bioimage analysis meets bioinformatics in spatial biology, and the role of imaging core facilities by 2030.

More details are on the [workshop page](https://www.globias.org/activities/globias2026-workshop-dortmund).


## Crick BioImage Analysis Symposium 2026

The seventh Crick BioImage Analysis Symposium (CBIAS 2026) takes place on 23–24 th November 2026 at the Francis Crick Institute in London. The two days of talks, poster sessions and panel discussions focusses on current topics in image analysis and image processing for microscopy. 

The symposium is followed by an 2-day napari workshop on 25–26 th November.
