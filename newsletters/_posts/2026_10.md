# Project highlight

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://biifsweden.github.io/images/newsletter/2026_10/ProjectHighlight.png" width="500" alt="Project image">
</p>

To study how wood anatomy changes with wind disturbances, **Kristína Lidayová** developed an image analysis workflow for confocal microscopy images of Safranin-stained wood samples. A FIJI macro preprocesses the images, finds the well-preserved wood regions, and segments the individual cells into lumen and cell wall. A Python Jupyter Notebook then detects the annual tree rings and for every cell it measures the lumen area and calculates the average wall thickness.

Explore the [code](https://github.com/ai4life-opencalls/oc_3_project_17)

## Images to Knowledge (I2K) 2026

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://biifsweden.github.io/images/newsletter/2026_10/I2K.png" width="500" alt="Project image">
</p>

The BIIF team, together with Leslie Solorzano from NBIS, held a virtual workshop titled "Integration of multi-modal data and visualization using TissUUmaps 4".
The workshop covered the basics of image registration, working with SpatialData, and aligning spatial transcriptomics data with light microscopy images. A final session showed how to visualize the registered multi-modal data in the newly released TissUUmaps 4.



## Elixir Spatial Omics course 

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://biifsweden.github.io/images/newsletter/2026_10/SpatialOmics.jpg" width="500" alt="Project image">
</p>

**Christophe Avenel** from BIIF co-organised the ELIXIR Spatial Omics Data Analysis course, held at BMC in Uppsala on 6–9 th October 2026, hosted by NBIS together with the ELIXIR Single-Cell Omics community. The four-day course brought together trainers from across Europe to teach participants how to analyse spatially resolved transcriptomics data. **Christophe Avenel** also gave lectures on data formats, cell segmentation and imaging-based spatial analysis, while **Jonas Windhager, Maximilian Senftleben and Suganya Sivagurunathan** helped with the practicals on data formats and cell segmentation. 

All the course materials are available on this [page](https://elixir-europe-training.github.io/ELIXIR-SCO-spatial-omics-2026/schedule.html).

# Publication highlight 

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://biifsweden.github.io/images/newsletter/2026_10/PublicationHighlight.jpg" width="500" alt="Project image">
</p>


Image from Eleanor M O´Roberts et al, 2026 [(CC BY)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/permissions/10.1080/01478885.2026.2703359?scroll=top)

 In this paper, O´Roberts et al. present a protocol that combines multiplexed detection of genes using DNA FISH with multiplexed immunofluorescence detection of proteins on the PhenoCycler Fusion platform in large tissue sections. **Christophe Avenel** from BIIF built the image analysis pipeline, which segments cells, measures protein marker intensities and counts DNA FISH spots in each cell. The data can be explored interactively in [TissUUmaps](https://is-pla.serve.scilifelab.se/TissUUmaps_project_1.tmap?path=dna_fish)!


Read the full publication here: [Eleanor M O Roberts, Pranauti Panshikar, Xiaoze Li Wang, Christophe Avenel, Quentin Verron, Eléna Coulier, Magda Bienko, Charlotte Stadler (2026). An integrated protocol for multiplexed DNA FISH and protein detection in large tissue sections. Journal of Histotechnology, 49(3), 199–215. https://doi.org/10.1080/01478885.2026.2703359](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01478885.2026.2703359)

Explore the [code](https://github.com/BIIFSweden/6870_DNA_isPLA)
