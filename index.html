The BioImage Informatics Unit helps you get the most out of your image data. Our experts support you with state-of-the-art image analysis, using anything from classical methods to deep learning depending on what your data needs. Whether you require a custom workflow developed for your specific project or training on existing software, we can help you get your analysis running.

To get started, reach out through our support request form and we will invite you to a free consultation.
